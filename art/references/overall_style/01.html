One last suggestion (this is something professional game teams do)

Create one more folder:

/art
    /concept
    /references
    /final
    /wip

  • concept/ → rough sketches and ideas (your building layouts, silhouettes, notes).

  • references/ → screenshots from games that inspire you.

  • wip/ → work-in-progress pixel art and experiments.

  • final/ → only approved assets that are actually used in the portfolio.

This prevents your project from becoming a mess of random images, and Claude Code always knows which assets are "official." As your world grows, you'll be very glad you kept your art pipeline organized from day one.
