# The Waterfront

**Art Direction Guide**

Version: 1.0

---

# Vision

The Waterfront is a handcrafted 2D pixel-art coastal town where every building represents a chapter of Fatima's software engineering journey.

The player should feel like they're exploring a quiet seaside town that has existed for years.

The world should feel:

* Cozy
* Nostalgic
* Lived-in
* Calm
* Honest
* Slightly magical

Never flashy.

Never chaotic.

Never overloaded.

---

# Visual Identity

## Primary Inspiration

Classic point-and-click adventure games combined with modern indie pixel-art lighting.

The world should immediately make someone think:

> "This looks like an indie game."

NOT

> "This looks like a portfolio."

---

# Art Style Rules

## Every object must have personality.

Nothing should look procedurally generated.

Buildings should lean slightly.

Wood should be uneven.

Stone should have imperfections.

Roofs should not be perfectly straight.

Paths should contain cracks.

Grass should grow unevenly.

The town should feel built over time.

---

# Pixel Style

Medium-resolution pixel art.

Not tiny retro pixels.

Not HD illustrations.

Characters:
48–64 pixels tall before scaling.

Props:
24–48 pixels.

Buildings:
Large illustrated pixel scenes with rich detail.

---

# Camera

Side-view (2.5D).

No top-down.

No isometric.

No first-person.

The player should always understand where they are.

---

# Environment

The waterfront should feel inspired by an old harbor town.

Materials:

* Weathered wood
* Brick
* Stone
* Copper
* Iron
* Glass
* Lanterns
* Rope
* Moss
* Ivy

Avoid futuristic materials.

---

# World Scale

Buildings should feel larger than the player.

Trees should feel taller than buildings.

The lighthouse should dominate the skyline.

The ocean should always feel endless.

---

# Color Philosophy

Use muted colors.

Avoid saturated "video game" colors.

Nothing should feel neon.

Every color should feel sun-faded.

---

Deep navy

Indigo

Purple

White floodlights

Blue

Lighthouse

# Shadows

Soft.

Long during sunset.

Blue at night.

Never pure black.

---

Foam

Reflections

Fish

Boats

Moon reflections

No still water.

---

# Sky

Large.

Open.

Calming.

Contains:

Clouds

Birds

Moon

Stars

Sun

Occasional shooting star

Never empty.

---

# Weather

Weather is information.

Sunny

Project completed.

Rain

Dormant.

Fog

Old memories.

Wind

Transition.

Storm

Difficult period.

Lightning

Breakthrough.

Dust

Abandoned experiment.

Confetti

Achievement.

Construction sparks

Current active work.

Never use weather only for decoration.

---

# Buildings

Every building must have:

Unique silhouette

Unique weather

Unique sound

Unique lighting

Unique animation

Unique interaction

Unique story

No two buildings should share the same personality.

---

# Windows

Windows reveal life.

Examples:

People working

Computer glow

Moving shadows

Coffee mug

Plants

Books

Blueprints

Never static black rectangles.

---

# Roads

Old stone paths.

Slightly uneven.

Small cracks.

Grass growing between stones.

Puddles after rain.

---

# Trees

Coastal trees only.

Wind-shaped.

Uneven.

Natural.

No perfect circles.

---

Wildflowers

Bushes

Small mushrooms

Driftwood

Rocks

Sea grass

Shells

Vines

Moss

Blueprint

Notebook

Backpack

---

# Animation Rules

Everything should breathe.

Subtle movement only.

Allowed animations:

Smoke

Water

Leaves

Flags

Birds

Clouds

Lantern flicker

Rain

Fog

Steam

Construction sparks

NPC walking

Boat drifting

Window glow

Forbidden:

Constant bouncing

Fast spinning

Excessive particles

Arcade-style effects

---

# NPC Design

NPCs should feel like townspeople.

Simple.

Readable.

Friendly.

Each NPC should have:

Idle animation

Walking animation

One line of dialogue

No complex AI.

---

# Character Design

Fatima

Silhouette should be recognizable.

Long dark outfit (respecting real-life appearance).

Simple backpack.

Laptop bag.

Comfortable shoes.

Neutral colors.

Readable at small sizes.

No exaggerated proportions.

---

# Dialogue UI

Inspired by classic adventure games.

Parchment texture.

Pixel borders.

Portrait.

Typing animation.

Simple choices.

No chat bubbles.

---

# Interface

The UI should feel handcrafted.

Wood.

Paper.

Brass.

Canvas.

Ink.

Avoid:

Glassmorphism

Blur

Modern SaaS cards

Floating shadows

Gradient-heavy buttons

# Screenshot Rule

Every screen should be beautiful enough to become a wallpaper.

If a screenshot looks empty,

add life.

If it looks crowded,

remove elements.

Balance always wins.

---

# Golden Rule

The player should never think:

"I'm looking at a portfolio."

They should think:

"I'm exploring someone's life, one building at a time."

Every artistic decision must support that feeling.
